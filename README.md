# Sentilytics
Sentilytics is an AI-driven sentiment analysis engine that translates text into measurable emotional intelligence. Feed it anything — a tweet, a customer review, a support ticket, or a long-form article — and it returns fine-grained polarity scores for positivity, negativity, and neutrality, an aggregated sentiment verdict, and an evidence-backed breakdown of the linguistic signals driving the result. Under the hood, transformer-based language models and targeted feature extractors combine to detect tonal cues (sarcasm, emphasis, hedging), sentiment-bearing tokens, and contextual modifiers, then weight them with confidence estimates to produce interpretable results suitable for both exploratory analysis and production pipelines.

Key capabilities:
- Polarity decomposition: calibrated scores for positive, negative, and neutral sentiment.
- Verdict synthesis: single-label sentiment decision with confidence.
- Explainability: token- and phrase-level attributions highlighting emotional drivers.
- Robustness: handles short messages, long-form text, and mixed-language inputs.

Sentilytics surfaces not only what people feel but why the model made that determination — turning raw text into actionable signal for product, marketing, research, and content professionals.
